Secure Broker™ is a new industry initiative focused on addressing data theft and encouraging responsible data handling in alternative finance. The program establishes a practical framework designed to reduce opportunities for misuse, distinguish responsible operators, and help small business owners pursue alternative capital with greater confidence and better outcomes.

Program Scope

Guidelines are for deal-processing roles (e.g. underwriters, sales, etc.)

The program focuses on protecting the initial submission package, where unauthorized use most often occurs. There is no specific software to buy or system to adopt. Many companies already have safeguards that may meet the standard, and if something is missing, the goal is simply to find a practical way to close the gap.

When the initial submission package can be downloaded from an email inbox or system of record, the program looks for reasonable controls that help reduce opportunities for those files to be moved outside the company.

Leadership

Program Director

Christina Duncan

Christina Duncan has worked in alternative business finance since 2009, beginning her career with a boutique equipment leasing firm. Over the following 15 years, she served as COO of two working capital brokerages, including one with an in-house funding division and more than 100 employees.

In 2025, she founded Aquamark in response to longstanding concerns around the handling and misuse of business funding applications. That work led to the development of Secure Funder™ and Secure Broker™, new independent certification initiatives designed to establish a practical industry standard and reduce opportunities for continued misuse.

Today, Christina’s work focuses on advancing transparency and accountability across the industry, advocating for measures such as broker registration, and helping create an environment where small business owners can seek alternative capital with greater confidence and better outcomes.
