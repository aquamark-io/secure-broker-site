Add it to Gmail

  1. Click Copy signature above (or select the signature with your mouse and press Ctrl/Cmd + C).
  2. In Gmail, click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new.
  4. Click inside the signature box and paste (Ctrl/Cmd + V).
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose it for new emails and replies.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
